Rules
1Three lines at most, at 13.5px. The rows have the detail; the summary is for reading the logic at a glance.
2The group's conditions share the first line after its operator (“All of:”). Each subgroup gets its own line, starting with the group's word: and, or, nor.
3Short phrases: “tagged Drone”, “4K”, “over 10 min”, “added in the last year”, “in Family” (the last folder name), “GoPro” in path. A None of reads “neither … nor …”.
4A subgroup is spelled out one level down only. Anything deeper is a token with its operator and number of conditions, all of 5; tapping it opens that group.
5Over three lines, whole items move into “+ N more”; tapping it shows the rest of the summary.
6Inside an opened group, the summary describes only that group. The breadcrumb says where it sits.
5bThe whole filter, just back from “All of”: that group is a row under Any of (it is three levels down), with a ring for a moment to show where you were.
